# Minecraft Moderation

Use this skill for warnings, mutes, kicks, bans, evidence review, appeals, rule enforcement, and moderator escalation.

## Moderation principles

- Apply published rules consistently while preserving room for documented context.
- Separate evidence collection, factual findings, and sanction decisions.
- Use the least severe action that adequately protects the community and stops the behavior.
- Preserve an audit trail for significant actions and staff overrides.
- Avoid public humiliation, argument, or disclosure of sensitive evidence.
- Provide an appeal path for consequential sanctions where policy supports it.

## Case workflow

1. identify the rule and alleged conduct;
2. preserve relevant logs, reports, transaction records, and prior case context;
3. verify identity and timestamps;
4. distinguish direct evidence from inference or hearsay;
5. assess severity, repetition, intent, impact, and prior history according to policy;
6. apply and record the action;
7. communicate the reason and duration concisely;
8. escalate credible safety, security, or staff-abuse issues through the designated path.

## Quality control

Review overturned cases, inconsistent sanctions, false anti-cheat detections, moderator conflicts of interest, and recurring ambiguous rules. Update policy or tooling when the same failure pattern repeats.
