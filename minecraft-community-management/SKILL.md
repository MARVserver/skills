# Minecraft Community Management

Use this skill for Discord/forum/community channels, announcements, feedback, onboarding, sentiment, and player communication.

## Operating model

Maintain clear channel purposes, rules, staff contact paths, announcement ownership, event calendars, known-issue communication, and feedback intake. Keep official decisions distinguishable from informal staff opinions.

## Communication rules

- State confirmed facts separately from plans or hypotheses.
- Give maintenance windows, expected impact, and follow-up status when service changes affect players.
- Do not promise dates or features that engineering/operations have not committed to.
- Summarize large feedback sets by themes and evidence rather than loudest voices.
- Close the loop when player feedback results in a decision or change.
- Avoid exposing private moderation/support details in public explanations.

## Community health

Track recurring confusion, churn reasons, new-player friction, toxic interaction patterns, unanswered support themes, event participation, and sentiment shifts. Use qualitative context alongside metrics; neither a single poll nor raw message volume represents the entire player base.
