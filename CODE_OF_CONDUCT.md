# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

MARVserver aims to keep this project technical, constructive, and welcoming to contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and technical trade-offs on their merits;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- assuming good faith while still challenging unsupported claims;
- respecting privacy, credentials, infrastructure details, and player data;
- correcting mistakes without personal attacks;
- keeping discussions relevant to the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments;
- deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated off-topic promotion;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- encouraging unsafe access to systems, credentials, or player data;
- sustained personal attacks or bad-faith review behavior.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki activity, and other project spaces operated by MARVserver.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially harms the project or its community. Moderation should be proportional to the behavior and documented where practical.

If a conduct concern should not be handled publicly, contact a MARVserver organization maintainer privately or use GitHub's built-in content reporting mechanisms when available.

Security vulnerabilities are handled separately under `SECURITY.md`.
